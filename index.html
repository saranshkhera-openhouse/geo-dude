        What happens to your photos

        1. Read from your device, into this tab only
        2. Converted to JPEG if needed, and resized
        3. Stamped with the society’s coordinates
        4. Saved straight back to your downloads

        No server, no account, no copy kept. Close the tab and nothing remains.

        How to use it

        1. Add your photos. Drag them in, or tap browse your files to pick them from your phone’s gallery. Add as many as you like — picking again adds to the list instead of starting over, and the same photo twice is ignored. Tap the × on any photo to drop it.
        2. Set the location. Type a few letters of the society name and pick it from the list. Or switch to Enter coordinates and paste a latitude and longitude — or a Google Maps link — if your society isn’t listed, or you want a more precise spot. Either way, the coordinates shown are what goes into every photo in the batch.
        3. Download. You get a single ZIP named after the society. Unzip it, and upload those photos to your listing.

        Questions

        Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?
        No. Everything happens inside this page, on your own device. There is no server to send them to — you can switch off your internet after the page loads and it still works.
        Why does 99acres reject my photos?
        Their listing form checks each photo for the GPS coordinates cameras store inside the image file. Photos taken with location switched off never had them. Photos forwarded through WhatsApp lose them, because WhatsApp strips that data when it compresses the image. Screenshots and downloaded photos don’t have them either.
        My photos are from an iPhone. Will they work?
        Yes. iPhones save photos as HEIC, which can’t hold GPS data in the way listing sites read, so they’re converted to JPEG here automatically. The first HEIC takes a moment longer while the converter loads.
        Will this make my photos look worse?
        Barely. Photos already under 2.5 MB are passed through untouched. Larger ones are scaled to 2560 pixels on the long edge — still sharp on any screen — and saved at the best quality that fits the size limit.
        My society isn’t in the list.
        The list covers 1,150 societies across Gurgaon, Noida and Ghaziabad. If yours is missing, you can add it to coords.json in the repository, or open an issue and it can be added.
        Can I set the location myself?
        Yes. Switch step 2 to Enter coordinates and paste a latitude and longitude, or a Google Maps link. Use it when your society isn’t in the list, or when you want a more precise spot than the society centre — a particular tower or block, say. If a society is already selected, the Not precise enough? link carries its coordinates across so you can adjust them instead of typing from scratch.
        To get the numbers: in Google Maps, right-click the exact spot and click the coordinates that appear to copy them.
        Is the location exact?
        When you pick from the list, every photo in the batch gets the society’s centre point, not the precise spot each photo was taken. Enter coordinates yourself if you need something more exact.
